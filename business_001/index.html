Bagel's Plus has been preparing its bagels the traditional way for over twenty-five years: boiled, then baked, and served fresh daily alongside lunch sandwiches, housemade hummus and Nova smoked salmon.

A neighbourhood bagel shop, kept simple.

Bagel's Plus keeps its focus narrow: bagels and croissants baked fresh daily, sandwiches for lunch, and plenty of cream cheese flavours to pair with them. Teas and coffees round out the counter, and the shop offers private lot parking and bike parking. Dogs are welcome.

The shop sits on East Fletcher Avenue in Tampa, a short drive from the University area, and has served the neighbourhood since 1998.

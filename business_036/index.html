Taqueria Chapala Jalisco brings the tacos of Jalisco to Lakewood — slow-cooked meats, house salsas and tortillas done the traditional way.

Taqueria Chapala Jalisco is a family-operated food truck that has been serving Lakewood since 2017. Visitors know it for Jalisco-style tacos and for two house salsas — chipotle and aguacate — that regulars recommend by name.

It is a takeout spot with private lot parking: order at the truck, take it home, and taste why neighbours keep coming back for the suadero.

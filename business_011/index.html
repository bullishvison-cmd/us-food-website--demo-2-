What people keep coming back for

These dishes are mentioned by visitors in public listings and reviews. They are not a verified full menu, and no prices are shown here.

Tacos

Street-style tacos with handmade tortillas — visitors mention grilled chicken, steak and chorizo fillings.

Pupusas

Salvadoran stuffed griddle cakes, praised by visitors with green salsa and curtido-style accompaniments.

Burritos

Generously filled burritos, including grilled chicken and carne asada versions visitors single out.

Sopes & Gorditas

Thick, hand-formed bases topped fresh — a heartier alternative to a taco.

Tortas & Quesadillas

Pressed sandwiches and melted-cheese quesadillas from the same fresh, made-to-order kitchen.

Drinks & Sweets

Visitors mention horchata, fresh fruit drinks, mango paletas and pineapple cookies.

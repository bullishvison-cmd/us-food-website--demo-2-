From the case

Glazed, by the dozen

Assorted glazed donuts are the most-mentioned order — big, soft and fresh, with surprise donut holes tucked into the box.

Filled to order

Chocolate long johns filled with Boston cream, maple-filled longs and other cream- and custard-filled donuts, filled on the spot.

Apple Fritters

Visitors call the apple fritter a standout — crisp outside, soft inside, and full of apple.

Bear Claws & Cream Horns

Bear claws are a repeat visitor pick, with cream horns and pastries also in the case.

Maple Bacon

Maple bacon donuts appear among the shop's wider variety of flavours visitors mention.

Boba, Coffee & Smoothies

Boba tea — honeydew is a visitor-mentioned flavour — plus custom coffee drinks and smoothies.

From the menu, as visitors describe it

What regulars come back for

Items below are dishes mentioned in public listings and visitor reviews, not a verified full menu. Ask in store for the current menu.

Beef pho

The house signature — beef pho with a long-simmered broth, praised by visitors for depth and consistency.

Chicken pho

A lighter bowl, frequently mentioned alongside the beef pho by Portland visitors.

Spring rolls

Fresh rolls and crispy rolls, both named in visitor reviews of the restaurant.

Grilled pork rice

Special rice with grilled pork, pork skin, and a Vietnamese egg-pork patty, as described in the restaurant's listing.

Vegetarian bowls

Tofu and vegetable bowls made with a separate vegan broth base simmered from radishes, carrots, and onions.

Bún bò Huế

Spicy beef noodle soup, singled out by visitors as a standout alongside the pho.

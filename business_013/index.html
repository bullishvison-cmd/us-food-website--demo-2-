Visitor-reported favourites

From the truck

These items are named in public listings and visitor reviews. This is not a verified full menu, and no prices are shown here.

Tacos

Handmade tortillas with asada, pastor, pollo, carnitas, chorizo, lengua, birria and more — served with onion, cilantro, radish, grilled onion, jalapeño and green or red sauce.

Torta al Pastor

A visitor favourite: pastor in a pressed torta, alongside other torta fillings.

Chorizo Burrito

Named among the truck's customer favourites, with burritos and burrito bowls also listed.

Quesadillas

Grilled quesadillas including a birria version visitors praise for its generous filling.

Gorditas & Sopes

Gorditas filled with meat, lettuce, tomato, sour cream and sauce; sopes also on the menu.

Street Corn & Carne Asada

Elote corn on the cob and carne asada plates served with beans, rice, salad, tortillas and grilled onions.

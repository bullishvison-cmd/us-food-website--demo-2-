About

Built on a plank, not a flat-top

DJ's Cast Iron Burgers is co-owned by longtime friends Miguel Cardona and Jose Nieves. The truck works with a deliberately simple menu: house-made ingredients, hand-cut fries, fresh vegetables, and never-frozen Angus beef, cooked on cast iron planks for a crisp outer crust and a juicy inside. Order one, two or three patties.

House rule

Order the double

The patties are deliberately thin, and the truck itself recommends ordering a double. The menu stays short on purpose, so every burger comes out the same way, every time.

Menu

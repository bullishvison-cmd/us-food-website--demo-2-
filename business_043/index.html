Our Shop

Downtown Spokane, since 2009.

A local shop, not a chain.

Atticus Coffee & Gifts sits in downtown Spokane, in the spot formerly home to 4 Seasons Coffee. Visitors describe a cozy, brick-walled room with outdoor seating — the kind of place where you order an espresso, pick up a bag of local roast, and browse the gift shelves on the way out.

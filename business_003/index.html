The quesadilla line-up

Signature quesadillas are served with black beans and cilantro-lime rice, with a choice of fresh toppings and house-made sauces. Items below come from the truck's published listings.

The Tony

The signature: four cheesy quesadillas with a side of black beans and cilantro-lime rice.

The Mama

Three cheesy quesadillas with black beans and cilantro-lime rice.

The Lil

Two cheesy quesadillas with black beans and cilantro-lime rice.

Chef Style

Four signature quesadillas: barbacoa, carnitas, shrimp and chicken, with a choice of cheeses.

By the filling

Barbacoa, carnitas, shrimp, chicken and veggie quesadillas, plus fried quesadillas.

And the rest

Nachos, burritos, queso dip and tortilla chips, plus fried eggrolls.

Sweet quesadillas

Fried guava and cream, strawberry and cream, and Nutella and cream.

To drink

Homemade sweet lemonade and bottled drinks.

Sides

Yellow rice and black beans, and fresh toppings with signature sauces.
